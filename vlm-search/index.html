Natural-language search over driving video

I fine-tuned a vision-language model so that millions of hours of driving footage can be searched by typing what you want to find. This is the search running against the full corpus — no pre-built tags, no manual labeling.

What's happening in the clip:

  • A plain-English query runs across the entire clip corpus in just over a second.
  • Thumbs up and down on individual results refine the query vector in place — relevance feedback, not a re-query.
  • Top-result similarity climbs from roughly 0.46 to 0.88 after a couple of rounds of feedback.
  • The refined vector gets a threshold, is saved as a reusable tag, and exports as a dataset for training.

    • Built on LoRA fine-tuning of NVIDIA Cosmos VLMs with a contrastive MaxSim late-interaction objective and hard negative mining. Evaluated against a human-labeled taxonomy using top-k precision and recall.
